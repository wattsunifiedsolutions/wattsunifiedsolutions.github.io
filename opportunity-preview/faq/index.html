Frequently Asked Questions

Understand the basics before you decide.

These answers cover the most common questions about experience, licensing, cost, time commitment, income, and the two partnership paths.

Do I need prior financial-services experience?

No. Prior experience can help, but it is not required. Regulated activity requires the appropriate state licensing, training, carrier appointments, and compliance before you can make recommendations or conduct business.

Can I start part-time?

Yes. Many professionals begin alongside an existing career or business. Your activity level should fit your availability, licensing status, and client-service responsibilities.

What does it cost to get started?

The initial registration is $199. State licensing and education costs vary by state. E&O professional insurance is approximately $35 per month. Additional background, carrier, or compliance costs may apply depending on your state and path.

Is income guaranteed?

No. This is not a salaried employment offer and income is not guaranteed. Licensed professionals are compensated according to eligible business, carrier contracts, licensing, and applicable program terms.

Do I have to recruit other people?

No. A professional can focus on personal production and client service. Developing licensed partners may be an option for those who want to grow that side of the business, but it is not the only way to build.

What is the referral-partner path?

It is designed for professionals who want to keep their primary profession first while creating a trusted relationship for people who may need protection, retirement, or legacy education. Any referral activity or compensation must follow applicable program terms and law.

What products and services are involved?

Depending on licensing and client needs, conversations may include life insurance, living benefits, final expense, retirement and rollover education, Fixed Indexed Annuities, estate-planning resources, and related protection strategies.

Who is a strong fit?

Relationship-driven professionals, veterans, business owners, educators, public servants, community connectors, and people who value education-first client relationships can be a strong fit. The private overview helps determine whether the model matches your goals.

What happens before I register?

You should review the opportunity, understand the costs and licensing requirements, ask questions, and decide whether the business-owner or referral-partner path fits before making a commitment.
