Starting Commitment

What business owners should expect.

  • $199 initial registration
  • State licensing and pre-licensing costs vary by state
  • Approximately $35/month E&O professional insurance
  • Background, carrier appointment, and compliance requirements may apply
  • Time is required for licensing, training, prospecting, follow-up, client service, and skill development

What this is not

This is not an employment offer, a guaranteed-income program, or a promise that everyone who starts will build the same business. Results depend on licensing, activity, skill development, market conditions, client needs, and many other factors.
